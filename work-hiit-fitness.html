The brief

"I want the best fitness app features all in one place — and I want it to feel like it was built for serious athletes."

Casey is a creative in Bridgend with a clear vision: the fitness world is full of apps that each do one thing well, and nothing that does everything. He wanted a platform that combined AI coaching, intelligent meal planning, a body composition scanner, full nutrition tracking, and a social layer where athletes could share their progress — all wrapped in a UI that felt as serious as the workouts inside it.

We built it across four platforms: iPhone, Apple Watch, Android, and Garmin — with a dark, high-contrast design language that matches the energy of the people who'd use it every day.

What's inside

Five tools.
One app.

AI Coaching

Personalised workout plans generated and adjusted by AI based on your fitness score, goals, and progress. The coach adapts as you do.

Meal Planner

Smart weekly meal planning that works alongside your training schedule. Fuel your workouts, not just your hunger.

Body Scanner

Track your physique over time with the built-in body scan tool. Honest progress tracking without the guesswork.

Nutrition Tracker

Log calories, macros, and meals. The tracker surfaces insights rather than just numbers — built to help, not overwhelm.

Social Feed

Share your workouts, stats, and milestones with a community of people who get it. Stories, trending posts, and real workout share cards.

HIIT Score

A single fitness score that combines activity, consistency and performance into one number — so you always know where you stand.
